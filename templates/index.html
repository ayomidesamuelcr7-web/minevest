How does MineVest generate daily yields on Bitcoin?

Yields are generated through high-efficiency green ASIC Bitcoin mining farms located in Iceland and Texas, combined with algorithmic market-making liquidity pools and cross-exchange OTC arbitrage.

When can I withdraw my accrued profits and capital?

Daily yield earnings can be withdrawn instantly at any time 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Minimum withdrawal is $25 USD equivalent in BTC. Initial invested capital is returned to your available balance upon plan maturity.
